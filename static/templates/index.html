NOTIFY ME

At Slyce, every property is held in a trust to protect investors and ensure fair distribution of rental income and growth. When you purchase a Slyce, you become a beneficiary of that trust. This trusted legal structure is widely used in property and business, giving you confidence that your investment is secure, transparent, and compliant.

  • The Trust - property is held separately for investors.
  • The Trustee - Slyce manages compliance, tenants, and property.
  • The Beneficiaries - investors earn rental income + capital growth.

You invest → The Trust owns → Slyce manages → You earn.

  • Management Fee: A 6% fee on rental income, covering tenant management, property maintenance, and trust administration.
  • Transaction Fees: A 2% fee applies when purchasing Slyces and a 1% fee when selling. This structure is designed to encourage long-term investing and help maximize compounding returns.
  • Transparent Pricing: All fees are clearly disclosed upfront — no hidden charges or surprises.

We keep fees simple and transparent so you always know where your money goes.

You can sell your Slyces directly through our platform. Once you decide to sell, your Slyces are listed on our marketplace for other investors to purchase.

  • Flexibility: Sell part or all of your holdings.
  • Timing: Sales are subject to market demand — typically faster than selling a whole property.
  • Value: Your sale price is based on the most recent property valuation and market activity.
  • Returns: If the property has grown in value, you keep the capital gains when selling.

This means you don't need to wait months or deal with agents — you can exit your investment when it suits you.
